# tori-tracker
Lightweight tool to track online fleamarket sales over one or more sales platforms

### The Problem
Selling used goods across multiple marketplaces (Tori.fi, TechBBS, Vinted, Huuto.net, Facebook Marketplace, etc) makes it difficult to keep track of active listings, compare platform velocity, and monitor annual sales revenue. Once an item is removed from a platform, its history and photos are lost.

### The Solution
`tori-tracker` acts as a central ledger:
- Save draft descriptions and listing links in one place.
- Track active items across different platforms.
- Record final sale prices, shipping fees, and profit margins.
- Aggregate annual revenue and profit metrics across all selling channels.
